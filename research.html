ESCALATION LAB · RESEARCH & PRIVACY

Research and privacy

Escalation Lab runs two kinds of simulation. FLASHPOINT:2027 is a public game that anyone can play. The private labs are code-gated research sessions run with partner institutions; at present that partner is the Belfer Center's Project on Managing the Atom at Harvard Kennedy School. This page says what each one does with your data.

◢ THE PUBLIC GAME

FLASHPOINT:2027 runs entirely in your browser. It stores nothing and sends nothing: there are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no server behind it. A run lives in memory until you close the tab. An after-action report exists only if you copy, download or print it yourself.

◢ THE PRIVATE LABS

You can only join a private lab with a code issued by the facilitator of a session. During a session the lab records:

  • the decisions and assessments a group enters, identified by the group's code;
  • a time stamp for each step;
  • survey answers, identified by a table number.

It never records names, email addresses or other identifiers. The app does not ask for them anywhere, and the facilitator will not enter them for you.

In some sessions, part of what a group reads is generated during the session by a model provider (Anthropic). To generate it, the lab sends the provider the scenario material together with the decisions and assessments groups have entered, their public statements and messages to the other side, any updates sent by the facilitator, and anything typed to an AI assistant; what comes back is logged with the rest of the session's data. This happens whether or not anyone uses the assistant. No names, table numbers or other identifiers are sent.

Participation is governed by the partner institution's ethics approval and consent process. How long session data are kept, and where, are set by that approval. You will be told what applies to your session, and asked for your consent, before it starts.

The lab's client code is published unobfuscated so that anyone can read exactly what the browser does.

◢ WITHDRAWING AND QUESTIONS

To withdraw, or to ask what was recorded for your group or table, contact Hallam Burnapp at h.burnapp.25@abdn.ac.uk, giving the session date and your group code or table number. Questions about a partner institution's ethics approval go to the contact named on your consent form.
