It's an exercise. Until it isn't.

Escalation Lab builds crisis simulations for teaching and research. Players take decisions under time pressure and incomplete information, and find out how quickly a crisis moves past the limits they meant to set.

An open crisis-origins exercise for seminars. Six players take seats at the table during a fictional crisis in July 2027 that runs across the Baltic, orbit and the Taiwan Strait.
